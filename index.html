How it works

Upload. Invite. Manage. Convert.

Four steps to make your database work at the trade fair — completely independent of the organiser.

01
Upload your database

Upload your entire prospect database — CRM contacts, marketing lists, target accounts. These are the people you've been chasing for months. Fingoh scores every one of them against your ICP using 41 intent signals, ranking them T1–T4 before you send a single invitation.

02
Invite your T1 and T2 prospects

Fingoh's Outreach Personalisation agent sends personalised invitations to your highest-intent contacts — inviting them to your booth, a dedicated meeting, or a product demonstration at the trade fair. Each invitation links to your Fingoh registration form, where prospects declare their interests, visit reasons, and whether they want a scheduled meeting.

03
Arrive knowing everything

By the time you walk into the venue, you know exactly who confirmed, what each prospect wants to see, and who to meet first. Your dashboard surfaces your invited prospects alongside walk-in visitors — all scored, all ranked, all with AI-generated talking points. Every conversation feeds back into your CRM with pipeline attribution.

Score decay built in: Pre-event scores decay 10%/week of inactivity · Onsite signals decay after the event ends · Post-event scores decay without new signals — ensuring the model always reflects current intent, never stale history.
